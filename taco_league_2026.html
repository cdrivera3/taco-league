Constitution
  • The 2/3 league vote will be used if anything must be decided. All members in the league must vote!
  • The league will operate within the guidance of the Sleeper platform.
  • Keepers: Each team can choose 3 keeper players from their team's prior years roster.
  • The first year's keeper fee is $5+ draft capital, followed by a second year's fee of $5+ draft capital. Minimum keeper base starts at $5 when calculating cost.
  • Waiver pickups can be kept for double the waiver price plus the keeper fee. If a player is traded or dropped, the base for keeping them is at their highest value. Minimum waiver pickup keeper cost starts at $10.
  • Players drafted, dropped/traded, and picked up again in the same year will not reset the keeper year count.
  • Player must be on the team prior to the final game of week 11 and for the remainder of the season to be keeper eligible. Player must have played at least 1 game to be keeper eligible.
  • Keepers must be identified a week before the next draft.
  • League Structure: Each team will have 15 roster spots: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 RB/WR, 1 TE, 1 K, 1 DEF, 1 IR, 5 Bench spots.
  • Standings tie breakers goes to points for (to lessen the effect of having a tougher schedule than other teams).
  • Playoffs start week 15 with 6 teams and the championship week 17.
