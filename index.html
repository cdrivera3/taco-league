🌮 Taco League 🌮

Draft is August 23, 2026. Keepers and buy-in are due August 9, 2026.
Constitution
  • The 2/3 league vote will be used if anything must be decided. All members in the league must vote!
  • The league will operate within the guidance of the Sleeper platform.
Keepers
  • Each team can choose three keeper players from their team's prior year's roster.
  • *The keeper function can only be used on a player two times consecutively.*
  • The first year's keeper fee is $5 plus draft capital, followed by a second year's fee of $5 plus draft capital. The minimum keeper base starts at $5 when calculating cost.
  • Waiver pickups can be kept for double the waiver price plus the keeper fee. If a player is traded or dropped, the base for keeping them is at their highest value. Minimum waiver pickup keeper cost starts at $10.
  • Players drafted, dropped or traded, and picked up again in the same year will not reset the keeper year count.
  • A player must be on the team prior to the final game of Week 11 and remain there for the rest of the season to be keeper eligible. The player must have played at least one game.
Pre-Draft Trading
  • Each team's three keepers must first be selected, locked, and paid for. After keepers lock, those players may be traded before the draft, along with any amount of auction draft capital agreed upon by the teams involved.
League Structure
  • Each team will have 15 roster spots: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 RB/WR, 1 TE, 1 DEF, 1 IR, and 5 bench spots.
  • Standings tiebreakers go to points for.
  • Playoffs start in Week 15 with 6 teams, and the championship is in Week 17.
